## Strategy Cards
A Strategy card may be played as either an Operations Card (OC) or as an Event Card (EC), designated a Future Operations card, or discarded. When played as an OC card, the player may perform one of the following actions:

A. Conduct an OC Offensive (7.0)  
B. Conduct a China OC Offensive (13.72)  
C. Withdraw an HQ (6.13)  
D. Bring an HQ into play from the game turn record track (6.15)  
E. Construct a strategic transport route (13.77)  


## 7.2 The Offensives Phase

1. The Offensives player activates supplied units within activation range of an eligible HQ (7.21). Any Offensives bonuses from the EC card that apply prior to movement are implemented (including receiving any reinforcements).  
2. The Offensives player moves activated units (7.22, 7.23).  
3. The Offensives player declares battle hexes (7.24).  
4. Reaction player may cancel the Offensive using a Weather Reaction Event. If the Offensive is cancelled, place all units to their starting location, remove event reinforcements, remove event replacements, and place the used Offensive card in the discard pile; return any ASPs used during the offensive; the offensive is concluded (7.28).  
5. Implement possible event bonuses from Offensive player’s EC card that apply prior to reaction.  
6. If Special Reaction is possible, Reaction player may roll to attempt creating additional battle hexes via Special Reaction (7.27). If, after this, no battle hexes were declared nor created due to Special Reaction, Reaction player may play any applicable Attack Reaction cards, then skip to Step 13, Post Battle Movement.  
7. The Reaction player attempts to change the Offensive’s Intelligence Condition by playing an appropriate Reaction card or, as an alternative, making an intelligence die roll (7.25). If, at the conclusion of this Step, the Offensive’s Intelligence Condition is Surprise Attack, skip to Step 9.  
8. The Reaction player activates and moves units to participate in battle hexes either declared by Offensives player or created by Special Reaction (7.26).  
9. Reaction player may play any Attack Reaction cards.  
10. Implement any offensive bonuses that apply after reaction. Implement any Attack Reaction cards that apply prior to battles.  
11. Players resolve all battles (7.28).  
12. Implement any Attack Reaction cards that apply after battles.  
13. Players conduct post battle movement (9.6). Reaction player first, followed by the Offensive player.  
14. Conduct all necessary emergency naval movement (8.22) and emergency air movement (8.32).  


## Reinforcement Placement
Reinforcements arrive according to the reinforcement schedule or due to an Event card. Ground or naval unit reinforcement must be placed in a friendly port hex where the unit is in supply and which is within activation range of an HQ that can activate the unit (6.12). Place an air unit reinforcement in a friendly airfield hex where the unit is in supply and which is within activation range of an HQ that can activate the unit. An HQ arriving as a reinforcement must be placed in a friendly port where it is in supply.

A reinforcement unit may never be placed in an un-neutralized enemy ZOI. HQs arriving as reinforcements on the current turn fulfill the supply and activation requirements for reinforcement placement only in the hex they occupy. To place reinforcements in other hexes, the supply and activation must be traced from an HQ that began the turn on the map.

Allied player places all reinforcements first, then the Japanese player. Placement of a reinforcement cannot alter enemy ZOI to allow other placements in the same segment; however, they may generate new ZOI to restrict enemy placement. Stacking and placement restrictions may not be violated.

Whenever a Chinese unit needs to be placed as if it were a reinforcement, it can only be placed in Kunming (2407).  


## Allied Replacements

### 11.31 Ground Replacements
The Allied player receives a specific number of ground replacements as per the Replacement Chart. The Allied player receives two ground replacements per game turn starting with game turn 2. Unused replacements are lost (they go to Europe). Allied ground replacements may be used for reduced or eliminated US and Commonwealth ground units.

### 11.32 Air Replacements
The Allied player receives 5 air replacements per game turn. These may be used for any reduced or eliminated Allied air units that can take replacements. Unused replacements are lost (they go to Europe).

### 11.33 Naval Replacements
The Allied player receives a number of specific naval replacements as per the Replacement Chart. Unused are lost.  
The Allied player gains one or two US naval replacements per turn (except turn 1) if Oahu (5808) is controlled. Each may be used for a reduced or eliminated eligible US naval unit.  
The Allied player gains one Commonwealth naval replacement on turns 6, 9, and 12 if any of Colombo (1307), Trincomalee (1308), Singapore (2015), Hong Kong (2709), or Townsville (3727) are controlled.

### 11.34 Chinese Replacements
If China has not surrendered, the Allied player receives one Chinese replacement on every odd-numbered game turn. It may be used to return an eliminated Chinese army at reduced strength to Kunming (2407) or to restore a reduced Chinese army to full strength. Kunming cannot be attacked and is always an available return location. A Chinese replacement may be placed in Kunming only if it is a supply source (13.75). Unused Chinese replacements are lost. Other replacements may not be used for Chinese units.

### 11.35 Dutch
There are no replacements for Dutch units. Once eliminated, they are permanently removed.

## Submarine Warfare
If 1d10 − current GT ≤ 0, Japanese draw is reduced by one card (minimum 4). In addition, the Japanese player permanently loses 1 Amphibious Shipping point and any Escort modifier is reduced from +4 to +2 or from +2 to zero.

### Submarine Warfare Modifiers
- Each Japanese escort event: +2  
- Allied player: +1 on all 1942 turns (defective torpedoes)  


## Strategic Bombing

### Procedure
Roll 1d10 for each B29.  
Full strength B29 succeeds on 0–8, fails on 9.  
Reduced strength B29 succeeds on 0–4, fails on 5–9.

### Effect
Each successful bombing reduces the Japanese Strategy card draw by 1 for that turn (minimum 4).

### B29 Losses
On any roll of 9, a B29 loses 1 step unless the Allies control an airfield within 5 hexes of Tokyo. Japanese High Altitude Interceptors add +1 to bombing die rolls until the Allies control a supply-eligible airbase within 5 hexes of Tokyo.  


## Supply Lines
A unit is supplied if an unblocked hex path can be traced from a supplied activating HQ to the unit and the path length does not exceed HQ range. A supply line may not cross:

A. An unplayable hexside  
B. A water hexside of an un-neutralized enemy ZOI  
C. Land hexside of a non-port coastal hex entered from sea  
D. Sea hexside of a non-port coastal hex entered from land  
E. Land hexside of a hex occupied solely by enemy ground, HQ, or air  
F. Sea hexside of an enemy-controlled port entered from land  
G. Land hexside of an enemy-controlled port entered from sea  


## 6.3 Unit Activation
A unit must be activated to move or attack (exceptions: emergency naval move, emergency air move, ground disengagement, retreat). The unit must have an activation path from the HQ used and be in supply (possibly from a different HQ).

An activation path may not cross:
- A water hexside of an un-neutralized enemy ZOI  
- A land hexside of a hex occupied solely by enemy ground or air  

Activation range may not exceed HQ command range. Unlike supply, activation may cross unplayable hexsides and switch between land and sea without ports.

Only one HQ per side may activate units per offensive; the number activated depends on card OC/logistics value and HQ efficiency (6.11).  


## 6.4 Aircraft Zone of Influence (ZOI)
All in-supply air and carrier units project a 2-hex ZOI. ZOI is neutralized if opposed by another ZOI in the same hex (except LRB). LRB units project ZOI but cannot neutralize enemy ZOI. Out-of-supply units have no ZOI.

Air ZOI is in effect when stationary and during non-strategic moves. Strategic movement loses ZOI until completed.

### Effects of an un-neutralized ZOI
- Blocks strategic movement entry/exit (8.23, 8.33)  
- Blocks amphibious assault entry/exit (8.44)  
- Blocks HQ activation path across all-water hexsides (6.3)  
- Blocks supply path across all-water hexsides (6.21)  

### Effects even when neutralized
- Offensives unit entering, exiting, or moving within Reaction ZOI: +2 to intelligence roll (7.26)  
- Amphibious landing in Reaction ZOI may allow Special Reaction (7.27)  

### ZOI does not affect
- Land-to-land ground movement  
- Naval or air movement other than strategic  
- HQ or supply paths across land hexsides  
- HQ range for maintaining reduced units in Attrition
